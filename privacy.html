Privacy

Short version: no cookies, no accounts, no selling your data. I count visitors without tracking who you are, and anything you type into the tools stays on your own device.

Last updated 11 November 2026

Who runs this site

BXTrades is my personal education site. I'm Boris, an engineering student in the UK. If you have any question about your data, email me at bxtrades1@gmail.com.

What I collect

Visitor counts. I use GoatCounter to see which pages get visited and roughly where visitors come from (for example TikTok or Google). It doesn't use cookies, doesn't follow you to other sites, and doesn't store your IP address. I only see totals, like "40 people opened the eval tracker today", never who you are.

Emails you send me. If you email me, I'll see your email address and what you wrote. I only use it to reply, and I don't add you to any list.

That's it. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no forms that send your details to me.

Your trades and settings stay on your device

The eval tracker, risk calculator, backtest journal and Candle Clash save what you type in your browser's own storage (called local storage), on your phone or computer. It never gets sent to me, and I can't see it.

  • It's like writing in a notebook that stays in your bag. Nobody else can read it, but if you lose the bag (clear your browser data or switch phone), it's gone.
  • Use the "Download backup" buttons in the tracker and journal now and then to keep a copy.
  • To delete it, use the delete buttons in each tool, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.
  • The site also saves a copy of its own pages on your device, so the tools open offline. That copy holds no personal information.

Other services the site uses

Some parts of the site load from other companies. When they do, your browser connects to them directly, and their own privacy policies apply:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, it may keep short-term technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security.
  • Google Fonts supplies the lettering.
  • TradingView shows the live chart and news calendar on the home page. Their widgets may set their own cookies.
  • Discord supplies the live member count.
  • TikTok and Discord links take you to those apps, which have their own privacy rules.
  • TradersScale affiliate link. If you click it, TradersScale may use a cookie to record that I referred you, so I can earn a commission. That happens on their site, not mine.

What I never do

  • Sell or share your data with anyone.
  • Run ads or advertising trackers.
  • DM you asking for money or to invest.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR) you can ask what data I hold about you, and ask me to correct or delete it. Because I barely collect anything, the answer will usually be "just your emails, if you sent any". Email bxtrades1@gmail.com. If you're unhappy with my answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Changes

If I add something new, like an email newsletter, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
