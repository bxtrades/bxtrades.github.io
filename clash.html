            How to play

            Everyone gets the same daily chart, so you can compare calls with friends. Read the trend, the range and where price sits inside it, then pick a direction. You get one guess per day, and your streak grows each day you’re right.

            Every chart is a real stretch of Nasdaq 100 futures (NQ) trading on a 5-minute, 15-minute or 1-hour timeframe. The date is hidden until you’ve made your call, so you can’t look up what happened.

            Practice mode gives you as many real charts as you like, with separate stats, so you can train your eye without touching your daily streak. You can pick a timeframe there too.

            Shaded bands mark the trading sessions in New York time: Asia (8pm to midnight), London (2am to 5am), New York morning (9:30 to 11am) and New York afternoon (1:30 to 4pm). Times along the bottom are UK time.

            In Practice you can switch to trade mode. Pick long or short, then drag the red stop line and green target line (or type the prices in) and place your trade. The reveal shows which gets hit first. Results are in R, where 1R is the amount you risked, so +2R means you made twice your risk. If one candle touches both, the game counts the stop first, to be cautious.

            Difficulty changes how many candles are hidden: 10, 20 or 40. The daily chart is always normal, so the leaderboard stays fair.

            To get on the leaderboard, pick a name after your first daily call. No account or sign-up needed: your name and streak are remembered on this device.

            The game plays short sounds when you lock in, as candles reveal and when you win or lose, and vibrates on phones that support it. Use the Sound button at the top to turn both off.

            After the reveal, use the replay controls under the chart to watch the hidden candles again one at a time. Press Play, step with Back and Next, or use the left and right arrow keys.

            Use the drawing tools to mark levels, trend lines and zones before you call it. Your drawings clear when a new chart loads.

            Turn on the range midpoint to see the middle of the visible high-to-low range. Price above it is in the upper half (premium), and price below it is in the lower half (discount).

            Charts use historical NQ futures prices. This is a training game, not trading advice.
