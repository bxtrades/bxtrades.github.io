FAQ

The questions I get most in my TikTok comments.

Are you profitable yet?

Not yet, and I won't pretend otherwise. I'm on a paper (simulated) account, practising for a 50k evaluation. Everything I post comes from that account or from backtests, and I post the losing days too.

Do I need money to start?

No. You can practise for free on a paper account. I use TradingView's paper trading. Only think about paying for an evaluation once you're consistent on paper.

Which prop firm should I pick?

The one whose rules you can actually live with. Compare the profit target, the max drawdown (and whether it trails), any daily loss limit, and the price. My risk calculator has common firms built in. I have a 40% off code for TradersScale, but that's an affiliate code, so don't pick a firm just because of a discount.

What are ES and NQ?

Futures contracts on the US stock market. ES follows the S&P 500 and NQ follows the Nasdaq-100. One point on NQ is worth $20 per contract and on ES $50. The micro versions, MNQ and MES, are a tenth of the size, which is why beginners often start with them.

What do all the words in your annotations mean?

FVG, SMT, AMD, liquidity sweep and the rest are all explained in plain English in the glossary, with links to my videos where I've made one.

Join the Discord. Each day there's a reminder post: call whether NQ closes up or down, and the leaderboard tracks who reads the market best.
